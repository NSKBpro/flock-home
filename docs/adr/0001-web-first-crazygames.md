# 0001 — Launch on the web, CrazyGames first

**Status:** Accepted, 18 Oct 2026

## Context

The project is a solo side project with a small time budget and no marketing money. Mobile stores need paid user acquisition to get players. Web portals bring their own traffic and handle ads. CrazyGames is free to publish, does not require exclusivity, and runs a two-week Basic Launch that measures engagement before any monetization.

## Decision

Ship first as a free HTML5 game on CrazyGames. Use Basic Launch as the go/no-go test. Consider other portals (Poki, itch.io, Playgama) and mobile only after CrazyGames data shows players stay.

## Consequences

- The first real signal comes from strangers, at no cost, within about a month.
- The game must be web-first: instant load, touch and mouse, works in a 16:9 iframe.
- Revenue depends on the portal's ad revenue share, which is not published and favours Tier 1 audiences.
- No in-app purchases at launch; CrazyGames offers purchases only by invitation to well-performing games.
